# Inspection review checklist (DRAFT, awaiting Cory's confirmation)

Written in plain words from Cory's expertise. No standard's text is used. ASTM D7338 is named only as a possible reference to authorize later (see LEGAL_REVIEW L20). Each question is asked of every area in a report. The result per question is: addressed, partly addressed, or not addressed. A gap becomes a leading question, never a grade.

## A. What was seen
1. Visible growth reported? Where, how much, on what material?
2. Staining, discoloration, or dark areas? Where?
3. Damage to materials (swelling, rust, rot, warping, crumbling)?
4. Hidden or blocked areas (behind cabinets, in wall cavities): opened or not?

## B. Moisture
5. Anything called wet or damp?
6. If so, measured? With what instrument?
7. Reading found, and the dry standard used to call it wet. Either (a) a dry standard taken on site (a reading on a similar dry spot of the same material) or (b) a generalized dry standard, stated by name or value. If neither is given, ask.
8. Source or cause identified, or evidence of one?
9. Current or historical moisture?

## C. Smell
10. Odor present? Where, what kind?

## D. Sampling reasoning
11. Why was each sample location chosen (the suspect condition it tests)?
12. Why not also the most accessible central point of the room, as a general-room reference?
13. For each surface sample: was a control sample taken from a similar surface with no suspect condition, to compare against?
14. For air samples: outdoor comparison present? Cavity or cabinet samples kept separate from room air?
15. Lab values placed under the right location (catches mislabeled locations).

## E. Scope and consistency
16. Areas inspected and areas not accessed.
17. Conclusions match what was seen and measured (no "no moisture detected" beside "chronic moisture" without explanation).
