# Intake questions for the report recipient (draft for Cory)

Rules (Cory, 2026-11-05)
- Every question has buttons. The client may pick more than one. Every question also has an optional text box.
- Everything is in the past tense: what led up to the inspection and what happened on the day. No blame, no questions about the inspector's character, no pricing.
- Every question allows "Not sure". Every section can be skipped and finished later.
- Health answers can be explained in the note box, but nothing in the review ever equates them to the report.
- Answers are the client's own statements. They are context for the review and never proof of a condition. Notes are untrusted text: capped, never read as instructions.
- The review uses answers to set what to expect in the report. If the client says X and the report is silent on the matching topic, the review asks about it (see the last table).

## 1. Your goal
1. What did you hope to learn from this report? [Whether it is complete · Whether the cause is right · Whether the recommended work is needed · What to do next · Something else]
2. What decision did you need to make? [Buy · Sell · Hire a remediation company · An insurance matter · Rent or lease · Keep living there · Something else]
3. What led to the inspection? [Saw visible mold · Saw staining · Smelled a musty odor · A water leak or flood · Buying or selling · Insurance asked for it · Routine check · Someone recommended it · Something else]

## 2. The home
4. Where was the property? [Florida · Another US state · Outside the US] (place in the note)
5. What kind of home was it? [Single-family · Condo or apartment · Townhome · Multi-family · Other]
6. About how old was the building? [Under 10 years · 10 to 30 · 30 to 50 · Over 50 · Not sure]
7. What was the foundation? [Slab · Crawlspace · Basement · Piers or pilings · Not sure]
8. Who lived in the home at the time? [My household · Tenants · Nobody (vacant) · Part-time use · Not sure]
9. How long had it been in that use before the inspection? [Under a month · 1 to 6 months · 6 to 12 months · Over a year · Not sure]

## 3. What was noticed
10. What had been noticed? [Visible growth · Stains · A musty odor · Damp or wet spots · Peeling or bubbling paint · Warped or soft materials · Condensation on windows · High humidity · Rust · Nothing, it was precautionary]
11. Where had it been noticed? [Kitchen · Bathroom · Bedroom · Living areas · Laundry · Closet · Attic · Garage · Crawlspace or basement · Around the AC unit · Exterior walls · Whole house]
12. When was it first noticed? [This week · This month · Past 3 months · Past year · Over a year ago · Not sure]
13. How had it changed? [Got worse · Stayed the same · Came and went · Got better · Not sure]
14. Did it follow a pattern? [After rain · In humid months · In cool months · After running the AC · No pattern · Not sure]
15. Had anyone in the home had health concerns they wanted to mention? [Yes · No · Prefer not to say] The note box lets them explain. Health answers are kept as the client's own words only. The review never links them to the report, to a finding, or to a cause, and no expectation row uses them.

## 4. Water history
16. What water events had happened? [Roof leak · Plumbing leak · Appliance leak · Window or door leak · Flooding · Storm or hurricane damage · AC condensation or drain overflow · Sewer backup · None known · Not sure]
17. When was the most recent one? [Past month · Past 6 months · Past year · Over a year ago · Not sure]
18. Had the water source been stopped by the time of the inspection? [Yes · No · Not sure]
19. What had been done to the wet materials? [Dried · Cleaned · Removed · Nothing · Not sure]
20. What had happened with mold in the home before? [Found before · Cleaned before · Professionally removed before · Passed a clearance test · Never · Not sure]
21. Had a water-damage insurance claim been made before? [Yes · No · Not sure]

## 5. The building and its systems
22. What cooling or heating did it have? [Central AC · Heat pump · Window units · Mini-split · None · Not sure]
23. Where were the air handler and ducts? [Attic · Crawlspace · Closet or utility room · Garage · Not sure]
24. Had the AC been serviced in the past year? [Yes · No · Not sure]
25. What AC or duct problems had there been? [Condensation on ducts · Clogged drain line · Dirty coils · Water at the unit · Weak cooling · None · Not sure]
26. Was a dehumidifier used? [Whole-house · Portable · No · Not sure]
27. Were exhaust fans used? [Regularly · Rarely · Not working · None installed · Not sure]
28. How was the AC usually run? [Always on · Turned up when away · Off for long periods · Not sure]
29. What work had been done before the inspection? [New flooring · New drywall or paint · Roof work · Plumbing work · New windows or doors · An addition · None · Not sure]

## 6. The day of the inspection
30. Was anyone living in the home that day? [Lived in as usual · Vacant · Partly used · Not sure]
31. Was the AC or heat running? [Yes · No · Not sure]
32. Were windows or doors open during the visit? [Yes · No · Partly · Not sure]
33. What was the weather? [Rain in the past 3 days · Dry · Very humid · Cool · Not sure]
34. Had the home been cleaned, painted or aired out just before? [Yes · No · Not sure]
35. Was the client present? [The whole time · Part of the time · No · Not sure]

## 7. What the inspector said
36. What was explained in person? [Findings in detail · Findings briefly · Sample results · Nothing · Not sure]
37. What was recommended? [Remove materials · Clean · Fix the source · More testing · Nothing · Not sure]
38. Had anyone else given an opinion or quote? [Another inspector · A remediation company · A contractor · No · Not sure]
39. How soon was the report received after the visit? [Same day · Within a week · Over a week · Not sure]

## 8. Limits (existing Q02 to Q10, put in the past tense)
Q02 limited in any way · Q03 service sold · Q04 time capped · Q05 samples capped · Q06 budget limited · Q07 areas included or left out · Q08 methods or openings declined · Q09 areas hard to reach · Q10 who proposed each limit (asked only if a limit was reported).

## 9. What you have and what is left
40. (Q11) Had other written records or verbal findings been given? [Yes · No · Not sure]
41. (Q13) Which records were received? [Full lab report · Chain-of-custody form · Field notes or field sheets · Calibration records · Signed scope of work · None of these]
42. (Q12) What question was still unanswered after reading the report? [Is the cause right? · Is the recommended work needed? · Was anything missed? · Are the results explained? · Something else]
43. Had a deadline been set? [Closing date · Insurance deadline · Lease or move date · No deadline · Not sure]

About 55 taps in total, 25 to 35 for a typical client after skips.

## What the answers let the review expect
| If the client said | The review looks for | If silent, the question is |
|---|---|---|
| Odor, or hidden-area signs (3, 10) | Hidden areas opened or explained | Which hidden areas were opened, and which weren't? |
| A water event (16, 17) | Water history in the report; source named | What water history was the cause compared to? |
| Source not stopped (18) | Source fix recommended first | What fixes the source first? |
| Mold before, or earlier removal (20) | Earlier work and clearance referenced | Was the earlier removal considered? |
| New flooring, drywall, paint (29, 34) | Hidden growth considered; materials opened | Were those materials checked? |
| AC problems (25, 28) | HVAC examined; duct or drain cause discussed | Was the AC system examined? |
| Vacant or long unused (8, 9) | Humidity and ventilation discussed | How did vacancy figure in the result? |
| AC off or windows open (31, 32) | Sampling conditions stated | What were the conditions during sampling? |
| Rain before the visit (33) | Weather noted with outdoor sample | Was the outdoor comparison taken that day? |
| Pattern with rain or AC (14) | Pattern considered in the cause | Does the cause explain the pattern? |
| Sample results not explained (36) | Results interpreted in the report | What do the results mean for this home? |
| Another opinion or quote (38) | Nothing; context for the reviewer | None |
