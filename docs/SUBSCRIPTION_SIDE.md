# Subscription side (inspectors): parked idea, not built

Status: idea only. Homeowner side ships first. Revisit with the attorney before any pricing is set.

## Who and why
Subscribers are inspectors who send their own reports for review before delivering them to the client. Homeowners know reports can be reviewed, so inspectors have a reason to be thorough up front. The aim is that reports get better until the service is needed less ("built to become unnecessary").

## Working rules (decided 2026-11-04)
- No grading, scoring, or ranking of inspectors. Nobody's price changes because of what a review found.
- Same price schedule for everyone. More reviews cost less per review, whatever the results.
- Private mirror: each subscriber sees only their own history of recurring question types. Never shared, no thresholds, never linked to pricing.
- Recurring questions become template fixes (for example fields in the IndoorIQ report builder), so they are fixed once.
- Graduation is rewarded: a cheaper "check before you send" tier, no penalty for stepping down.
- Extra help is offered on request or by volume, never triggered by how a report scored.
- Marketing line (public and subscribers): the job of this service is to become unnecessary as reports get better.
