# Lou's interview: draft question set (for Cory to review as a whole)

Rules: friendly, plain words, no blame, no guessing from price or length. Every answer is saved with the case (kept two years). Questions ask what happened, not who was at fault. Nothing here is built except the ones marked BUILT.

## Before payment (short, confirms what the visit covered)
- BUILT Q01 What did you hope to learn from this report, and what decision did you need to make?
- BUILT Q02 to Q09 Was the inspection limited in any way? What service was sold? Time, samples, budget, areas, methods, hard-to-reach areas (yes / no / not sure).
- BUILT Q15 Anything in writing before the visit (contract, estimate, email)?
- BUILT Q14 For each limit, who set it, and what led to it?

## After payment, while the reading runs (do not depend on the reading)
- BUILT Q16 Did the inspector tell you anything out loud about what they would or wouldn't check?
- BUILT Q17 Buttons: what they would check / would not check / how long / cost or package limit / areas that could not be reached / findings out loud / something else / I don't remember.
- Follow-ups for each button (each is a short typed answer, shown only if the button was tapped):
  - Would check: "What did they say they would look at?"
  - Would not check: "What did they say they wouldn't look at? Tell it the way you remember it."
  - Visit length: "How long did they say it would take, and about how long were they there?"
  - Cost or package: "What did they say the price or package included, and what was left out?"
  - Could not reach: "Which areas did they say they couldn't get to, and what was the reason they gave?"
  - Findings out loud: "What did they tell you they saw or found? Even a rough memory helps."
  - Something else: "Tell us what you remember."
- Why this inspection happened: "What made you get this inspection? For example buying, selling, a smell, a leak, insurance, or a health concern." (Pick one or more.)
- The visit itself: "Were you there for the visit? About what date was it?"
- Who else was there: "Was anyone else there, like an agent, a seller, or a contractor?" (yes / no / not sure; yes opens a short typed line)
- What you were shown: "Did the inspector show you anything during the visit, like a moisture reading or a photo?"
- What you received: BUILT Q13 which records came with the report (lab report, chain of custody, field notes, calibration, signed scope of work).
- Samples: "Did the inspector take any samples? Do you know where they went?" (yes / no / not sure)
- What the report told you: "Did the report match what the inspector said at the visit?" (yes / no / not sure; no opens a short typed line, asked neutrally)
- Earlier history: "Has this home had mold, water damage, or another inspection before? What do you know about it?" (typed)
- What has happened since: "Have you talked with the inspector since you got the report? What did they say?" (yes / no; yes opens a typed line)
- What you still wonder: BUILT Q12 What question was still unanswered after you read the report?

## After the reading finishes (capped at 5 questions)
- Lou asks only questions that came from what the reading found, most important first. Each says which page it came from and why it matters, in the same neighborly voice. This is the existing ranked question list, cut to 5 here.

## Closing
- "Is there anything else you think we should know?" (typed, optional)
- Lou thanks the client and points to the marked-up copy and the questions to take to the inspector.

## Never asked
- Who made a repair or when (decided by Cory).
- Anything about the inspector's license, honesty, or motives, and anything that guesses from price or length.
